Your accountant just became an AI AI — Claude is now baked into Xero's bookkeeping software

## MARKET REACH

Claude now has access to **3.5M** businesses using Xero globally. This integration puts Anthropic's AI directly into the daily workflow of millions of small business owners who've never touched ChatGPT. No new login, no separate tool—it's just there when they open their books.


## 01. Invoice reconciliation in plain English

Ask Claude to categorize three months of uncoded transactions. It reads receipt images, matches vendors, suggests expense categories based on your chart of accounts. What took 4 hours now takes 11 minutes.


## 02. Tax prep without the spreadsheet archaeology

Claude pulls end-of-year reports, flags missing 1099 data, and drafts accountant handoff notes. It knows which expenses are deductible in your state. Your CPA gets a clean packet instead of a shoebox.


## Before vs. after Claude after

**OLD XERO**

- Email your accountant with questions
- Google tax rules per state
- Export-import CSV hell

**XERO + CLAUDE**

## The embedded AI land grab embedded

- OpenAI is chasing consumer buzz. Anthropic is embedding Claude in enterprise SaaS where people actually work.
- Xero competes with QuickBooks and FreshBooks—both now scrambling to add AI or lose customers.
- This integration means Anthropic gets usage data from real accounting workflows, training Claude on what small businesses actually need.

*AI wins are shifting from chat apps to the tools you already pay for.*
