## 01. The News: Grok's Liability

SpaceX's S-1 filing for its IPO listed Grok's "spicy" outputs as a potential risk factor. This is not a content moderation debate; it's a financial disclosure.


## Operational Risk — *noun.*

The potential for losses resulting from inadequate or failed internal processes, people, and systems, or from external events.

> An AI model generating unpredictable or legally problematic content, leading to reputational damage or regulatory fines.


## The Actual Delta: What This Changes

- Unpredictable AI outputs carry tangible business risk, not just user complaints.
- "Fun" features like "spicy" mode are liabilities in regulated contexts.
- Operationalizing AI means engineering for predictability, not just capability.

*Demos lie; shipped things don't.*
