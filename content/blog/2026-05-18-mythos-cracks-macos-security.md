Your Mac Is Safe. Unless Anthropic's _Mythos_ Is Involved. Mythos — Researchers just demonstrated how an LLM can find macOS vulnerabilities.

## 01. LLM Cracks macOS

Security researchers used Anthropic's Mythos LLM to identify a zero-day vulnerability in macOS. This wasn't theoretical; it was a concrete exploit found by an AI.


## Mythos — *noun.*

Anthropic's research-oriented LLM, designed for advanced reasoning and complex problem-solving, often used for internal safety and alignment work.

> Researchers applied Mythos to macOS security, yielding unexpected results for a 'safety-focused' model.


## AI-Assisted _Red Teaming_ Red Teaming

- Researchers prompted Mythos to analyze macOS system code.
- The LLM identified a logic flaw in a core system service.
- This flaw allowed for privilege escalation, a critical security breach.

*The AI didn't just 'help'; it *found* the exploit.*


## SAFETY VS. EXPLOIT

The company behind Mythos, Anthropic, is a leading voice in AI safety. Yet, their own model was instrumental in a major exploit. **100%** of the exploit's core logic was identified by Mythos, not just human input.. This raises uncomfortable questions about AI's dual-use potential, even from 'safe' models.


## Why This _Matters_ Now Matters

- **Apple Security** — Highlighting the evolving challenge of LLM-assisted vulnerability discovery. Patching will get harder.
- **AI Developers** — Emphasizing the unpredictable nature of advanced LLMs, even those built with safety in mind. Dual-use is real.
- **Enterprise IT** — Suggests AI could soon be weaponized for sophisticated attacks, requiring new defensive strategies.
