> We must not let a non-European actor scan military code bases. It would be a loss of sovereignty.

## Project Mythos — *noun.*

Anthropic's initiative to train AI models on vast, sensitive datasets, potentially including government and military code.

> Aimed at building highly capable, context-aware AI assistants.


## Why Sharing Data With *Non-EU* AI Is Risky Non-EU

- Proprietary data could be integrated into models accessible elsewhere.
- Loss of control over sensitive national defense information.
- Potential for foreign influence on critical infrastructure AI.

*A direct challenge to European digital sovereignty.*


## This Isn't Just About *France* France

- **Defense Contractors** — Need clear guidelines on what data can be shared and with whom for AI development.
- **AI Developers** — Must navigate complex geopolitical lines when sourcing data for model training.
