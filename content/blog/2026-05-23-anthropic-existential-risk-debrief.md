## 01. The headline you saw

Anthropic's co-founder recently stated there's a 'non-zero chance' AI could pose an existential threat. This isn't a new technical finding, but a philosophical stance reaffirmed.


## Existential Risk — *noun.*

A hypothetical scenario where advanced AI leads to human extinction or irreversible collapse of civilization. Often discussed in philosophical, not engineering, terms.

> This concept guides long-term research safety, but rarely informs weekly sprints.


## It *doesn't* change your roadmap. doesn't

- No new model capabilities or limitations shipped.
- No immediate impact on agent design or deployment.
- No reason to pause feature development or testing.

*The core work of building reliable AI remains.*


## Focus on *tangible* risks. tangible

- Data privacy and security vulnerabilities.
- Bias detection and mitigation in model outputs.
- Robustness: how models handle adversarial inputs.

*These are the problems we can ship solutions for.*
