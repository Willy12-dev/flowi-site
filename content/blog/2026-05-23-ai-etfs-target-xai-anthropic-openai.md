## 01. The "AI ETFs 2.0"

Harbor Capital launched new "Lab" funds. These aren't passive indexes; they're high-conviction bets on specific, private AI companies like OpenAI, Anthropic, and xAI.


## Lab Fund — *noun.*

A speculative investment vehicle focusing on a concentrated portfolio of early-stage, often private, companies within a high-growth sector. Implies higher risk and potential for outsized returns.

> The new Harbor Capital 'Lab' funds target pre-IPO AI giants.


## Why *these three*? these three

- OpenAI: The clear leader in foundational models, setting the pace for API-first development.
- Anthropic: A strong competitor, focused on safety and enterprise adoption with Claude.
- xAI: Elon Musk's venture, aiming for distinct niches with Grok and challenging incumbents.

*These are the names currently driving significant market attention and technical innovation.*


## THE ACTUAL DELTA

This means a new wave of **speculative capital** is directly tied to the performance of your core AI tool providers.. For builders, this implies increased pressure for rapid development, feature releases, and a sharper focus on monetizable outputs from these companies.


## Who *should care*? should care

- **AI Founders** — Recognize the market's appetite for specific AI niches and how it impacts valuation and partnership opportunities.
