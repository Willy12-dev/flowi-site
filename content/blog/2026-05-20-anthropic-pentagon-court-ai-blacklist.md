## 01. The Core Conflict

Anthropic filed suit against the DoD. They claim the Pentagon effectively blacklisted them from certain contracts, citing 'national security' concerns over data access and model transparency.


## Operational Blacklist — *noun.*

When an organization, often governmental, implicitly or explicitly bars a vendor from bidding on or securing contracts due to policy, trust, or perceived risk, not technical performance.

> The Pentagon's alleged exclusion of Anthropic from specific AI projects despite technical capability.

## Step 01: This Is Not About Model Quality

The legal challenge isn't about Claude's capabilities or safety features. It's about the *vendor relationship* and the terms under which AI is deemed 'deployable' in sensitive environments.
