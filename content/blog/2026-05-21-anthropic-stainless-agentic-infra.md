Everyone missed the real reason Anthropic bought Stainless. — Why agent reliability, not just competition, drove the deal.

## Stainless — *noun.*

A startup specializing in LLM-native data infrastructure, designed to manage, structure, and ensure the reliability of data for complex agentic workflows.

> Crucial for systems where agents interact with external tools or databases, ensuring consistent outputs and error recovery.


## Step 01: Agent demos look great. Production agents often break.

The unglamorous truth: building reliable AI agents means dealing with messy data, inconsistent tool calls, and state management. This is where most agent projects falter.


## Operationalizing agent reliability

- Structured data handling for LLM inputs and outputs.
- Robust tooling for agent state management and error recovery.
- Reduced hallucination and failure rates in complex agentic chains.

*This is the plumbing that makes agents dependable, not just flashy.*


## Who should care about this right now?

- **AI Builders** — If you're shipping agents, you know the pain of unreliability. This signals a need to invest in your own data infrastructure.
- **AI Founders** — Your first agent customers will demand consistency. Don't scale until you've systematized these core data challenges.
- **Dev Leads** — Evaluate your current agent stack. Are you building custom data layers or relying on ad-hoc solutions? There's a better way.
