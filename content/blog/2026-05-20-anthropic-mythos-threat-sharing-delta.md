## Project Mythos — *noun.*

Anthropic's confidential AI threat-sharing forum for industry leaders. Established to coordinate on severe AI safety risks.

> Members share vulnerabilities and mitigation strategies for frontier models *before* public disclosure.


## 01. The Original Secrecy

Mythos operated on a strict "need-to-know" basis. Information about shared threats was tightly controlled, even among members.


## 02. The Policy Shift

Anthropic now permits broader sharing of Mythos intel *within* member organizations. This means more researchers can access threat data.


## What This *Actually* Does Actually

- Faster internal threat dissemination for member companies.
- Reduced friction for security teams building with frontier models.
- Potentially quicker patch cycles for identified vulnerabilities.

*It's not a public API, but it means more robust, distributed security.*


## THE UNGLAMOROUS PART

This isn't about public access. It's about **1.2x** faster internal security loops for large AI teams.. The impact is in the internal operational efficiency, allowing member organizations to harden their systems quicker.
