## 01. The Exodus: 50+ Gone

Since February, over 50 employees have departed SpaceXAI, the merged AI arm of Elon Musk's empire. This isn't just attrition; it's a significant brain drain.


## Four Reasons Staff *Walked* Walked

- Burnout from intense project deadlines.
- Leadership changes post-merger creating friction.
- Aggressive poaching by rival AI firms.
- Liquidity events reducing long-term incentives.

*TechCrunch AI reports a confluence of factors.*


## Liquidity Event — *noun.*

When shares or ownership stakes become convertible to cash (e.g., IPO, acquisition, secondary sale). Often reduces incentive to stay if wealth is realized.

> Early SpaceXAI employees saw a liquidity event, then left.


## Who Should *Care*? Care

- **AI Founders** — Retention issues at a high-profile firm highlight the fragility of post-merger talent. Strong culture matters more than ever.
- **AI Engineers** — This signals potential red flags about work environment and long-term career growth in certain fast-moving ventures.


## The Broader Picture

High-growth AI firms average **18%** annual turnover. SpaceXAI's rate is more than double that.. While AI talent is fluid, 50+ departures in three months suggests systemic issues beyond typical industry churn.
