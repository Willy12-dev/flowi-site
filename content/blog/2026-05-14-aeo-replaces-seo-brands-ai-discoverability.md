## AEO — *noun.*

Answer Engine Optimization. The discipline of making your brand discoverable, trusted, and recommended by AI systems like Perplexity, ChatGPT, and Gemini.

> When someone asks Perplexity "best project management tool for remote teams," AEO determines if your brand appears in that answer.


## THE SHIFT

By mid-2026, **47%** of product discovery happens in AI chat interfaces, not Google.. Trustpoint Xposure became the first firm to formalize AEO methodology. Traditional SEO keywords don't matter when AI models synthesize answers from unstructured sources.
