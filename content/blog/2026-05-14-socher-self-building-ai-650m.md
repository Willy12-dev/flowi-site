The AI pioneer behind You.com and Salesforce AI is building a new startup. Its mission: an AI capable of researching, developing, and improving its own architecture indefinitely.


## Recursive Self-Improvement — *noun.*

An AI system designed to understand, modify, and enhance its own source code or training processes, leading to continuous, autonomous upgrades without human intervention.

> Imagine an AI that writes better code for itself, then uses that better code to write even *better* code.
