Anthropic's new agent finds bugs faster than developers can *patch* them. patch — The unglamorous part of AI agent deployment.

## 01. Mythos: The AI Bug Hunter

Anthropic's Claude Mythos Preview is an AI agent designed to autonomously explore codebases, identify vulnerabilities, and generate detailed bug reports. It's built to accelerate the QA process.


## THE ACTUAL DELTA

Mythos generates **MORE BUGS** than dev teams can fix.. The core warning from Anthropic: Mythos is so effective at finding issues that it's creating a backlog faster than human engineers can address them. This is the operational bottleneck.


## Beyond the Hype: Operationalizing Bug Agents Operationalizing

- Not a 'set and forget' solution for QA.
- Requires a re-evaluation of your bug-fix pipeline.
- Shifts focus from discovery to triage and remediation.

*The challenge moves from finding bugs to managing their volume.*


## Who Wins (and Who Doesn't) Wins

- **WINNERS** — Teams with mature devops, automated testing, and robust triage systems. They can absorb the AI's output.
- **STRUGGLERS** — Teams with manual processes, small dev teams, or existing backlogs. Mythos will amplify their pain points.


## Step 01: Before You Deploy Mythos

Before deploying, audit your current bug-fixing throughput. Implement AI-driven triage (e.g., categorizing, prioritizing). By Friday, identify your team's current bug resolution rate. This is your baseline.
