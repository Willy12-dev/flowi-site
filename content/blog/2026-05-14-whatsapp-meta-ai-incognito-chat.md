WhatsApp AI now has an incognito mode incognito — End-to-end encrypted chats that leave no trace

## 01. What actually changed

WhatsApp users can now toggle incognito mode for Meta AI conversations. When active, chats are end-to-end encrypted, Meta AI forgets everything after the session ends, and nothing feeds back into training data. It's a manual opt-in for every conversation.


## THE NUMBERS

Over **2.8B** WhatsApp users now have access. Meta rolled this out globally across WhatsApp's entire user base. That's more privacy-conscious AI conversations than any other platform offers at scale.


## Regular vs incognito mode incognito

**Regular Mode**

- Chat history saved
- Used for model training
- Personalized responses
- Persistent context

**Incognito Mode**

- No chat history
- Zero training data
- Generic responses
- Session-only memory


## What Meta didn't advertise advertise

- Incognito mode isn't default — you manually toggle it per chat
- Most users won't know it exists unless they dig into settings
- Switching mid-conversation resets all context immediately

*Privacy by obscurity, not design.*


## Who this actually matters for matters

- **Health queries** — Asking Meta AI about symptoms or sensitive medical questions without creating a permanent record
- **Financial advice** — Getting quick calculations or explainers on loans, taxes, or budgets that stay ephemeral
- **Relationship questions** — Venting or exploring tough personal decisions without Meta storing the context
