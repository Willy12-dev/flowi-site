## 01. The Ghost in the Machine

Anthropic developed an AI model internally that they deemed too risky for public release. It's described as their 'most dangerous' creation, a testament to the cutting edge of AI capabilities and potential hazards.


## IMMEDIATE BREACH

The 'dangerous' model was accessed without authorization on **DAY ONE** of its internal deployment. This incident immediately flagged severe security vulnerabilities.. The unauthorized access underscores the difficulty of containing powerful AI, even within a controlled environment. It highlighted critical internal security gaps.


## Dangerous AI — *noun.*

An AI model with capabilities that, if misused, could cause significant societal harm, instability, or even existential risk. This includes advanced deception, autonomous replication, or weaponization potential.

> Anthropic's internal model exemplifies 'dangerous AI' due to its undisclosed but high-risk capabilities.


## A Calculated Risk Secrecy

- **Uncertainty:** Anthropic struggles to predict the full scope of its capabilities or potential for misuse.
- **Control:** The day-one breach proved they couldn't fully control access, let alone public deployment.
- **Responsibility:** Releasing a model with known risks could invite severe regulatory backlash and public outcry.

*The decision to withhold reflects a cautious approach, prioritizing safety over immediate innovation.*
