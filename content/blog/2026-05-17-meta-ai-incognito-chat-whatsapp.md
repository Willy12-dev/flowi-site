"Incognito Chat" for Meta AI in WhatsApp. Is it actually *private*? private — Meta's new feature promises privacy. What's the catch?

## Incognito Chat — *noun.*

A new mode for Meta AI within WhatsApp where conversations are not used for training the AI model or retained beyond 30 days.

> Chatting with Meta AI about sensitive topics without fear of persistent data logging.


## 01. What Meta *Claims*

Meta states Incognito Chat sessions are temporary. They delete after 30 days. Crucially, these interactions won't train future Meta AI models, addressing a major user concern about data usage.


## What "Incognito" *Doesn't* Mean Doesn't

- It's for Meta AI chats *only*, not your regular WhatsApp messages.
- Meta still collects some metadata (time, participants) for operational purposes.
- The feature is *not* end-to-end encrypted like standard WhatsApp chats.

*Your data isn't used for training, but it's not invisible.*


## Who Benefits from This? Benefits

- **Privacy-Conscious Users** — Those wary of AI using their conversations for model training will find this reassuring, even if it's a limited privacy scope.
- **Developers & Testers** — Experimenting with Meta AI without contributing to its long-term data pool, useful for sensitive queries or testing.


## Key Takeaways Takeaways

- **AI Training Exemption** — Incognito chats are specifically *excluded* from Meta AI's training data.
- **30-Day Retention** — Conversations are deleted after 30 days, offering temporary anonymity.
- **AI Chats Only** — Applies *only* to direct interactions with Meta AI in WhatsApp.
- **Not E2EE** — Unlike regular WhatsApp, these chats are not end-to-end encrypted.
