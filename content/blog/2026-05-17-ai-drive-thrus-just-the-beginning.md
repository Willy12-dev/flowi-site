## Conversational AI — *noun.*

A system designed to interact with humans using natural language, typically via voice or text, to perform tasks or provide information.

> Taking a drive-thru order or answering common customer queries without human intervention.


## McDonald's paved the way. trials

- **2021** — McDonald's began its AI drive-thru trials.
- **100+** — Restaurants tested the technology by 2023.


## Wendy's and others join in. expanding

- Wendy's partnered with Google Cloud for its "FreshAI" system.
- Pilots aim to handle entire order flows, from greeting to payment.
- Goal: reduce wait times, improve order accuracy, free up human staff.

*This isn't unique to fast food.*


## Who benefits from AI service? Efficiency

- **RESTAURANTS** — Lower labor costs, consistent service quality, faster transaction times, especially during peak hours.
- **CUSTOMERS** — Shorter lines, potentially fewer order errors, and less pressure on human staff leading to better overall interactions.


Dear Service Economy,

The drive-thru is a proving ground. What starts as a simple order-taker evolves into a sophisticated front-line agent. Expect this integration to accelerate, moving beyond fast food to retail, banking, and beyond. This isn't just about speed; it's about redefining the human role in customer interaction.
