## 01. The Privacy Mandate

Apple built its brand on privacy. Now, with AI, that same ethos is driving a major Siri overhaul. It's a direct counter-move to data-hungry competitors.


## Siri's Data Diet Diet

- Chats could auto-delete after use.
- Less data stored on Apple's servers.
- User control over data retention.

*A clear differentiator in the AI assistant market.*


## Ephemeral AI — *noun.*

An AI system designed to minimize data retention, where interactions are temporary and automatically deleted to enhance user privacy and reduce data exposure risks.

> Siri's potential auto-delete feature, ensuring conversations don't persist indefinitely.


## THE BIG PICTURE

By prioritizing privacy, Apple aims to capture **60%** of users concerned about AI data handling, carving a distinct market niche.. While other platforms chase scale, Apple banks on trust. This move is less about raw power and more about user confidence.
