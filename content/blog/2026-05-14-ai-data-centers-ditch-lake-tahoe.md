## 01. The Unseen Power Grab

An energy supplier in Lake Tahoe diverted power from local homes to serve data centers. This isn't a fluke; it's a stark example of how AI's insatiable demand for electricity is reshaping infrastructure priorities.


## Energy Colonialism — *noun.*

The practice where high-demand tech industries (like AI data centers) consume disproportionate energy resources, often at the expense of local communities' stability and access.
