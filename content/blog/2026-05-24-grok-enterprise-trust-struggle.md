## 01. The Brand-Trust Mismatch

Grok built its identity on edgy, contrarian responses. This drives engagement but fundamentally conflicts with enterprise requirements for predictability and stability in production systems.


## Trust Stack — *noun.*

The layers of reliability, security, data governance, and support an AI product must demonstrate to be operationalized within a business environment.

> "A strong trust stack is more critical than raw model performance for enterprise adoption."


## Where Grok Falls *Short* Short

- Unpredictable outputs: Sarcasm and "takes" are not features in enterprise.
- Data handling: Lack of clear, robust data privacy and security guarantees.
- Support: Enterprise-grade SLAs and dedicated support channels are absent.

*These are the unglamorous parts that decide adoption.*
