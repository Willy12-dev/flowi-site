Everyone's watching global tensions. Few are tracking the *real* AI impact. real — Geopolitics isn't just news. It's a supply chain signal.

## 01. The Unseen Ripple Effect

Reports of an "Iran war shock" signal global instability. For AI, this isn't just a headline—it's a direct input into the operational environment for chips, funding, and talent.


## Three Direct Builder Consequences Direct

- **Chip Supply**: Expect increased lead times and potential cost hikes for critical hardware.
- **Funding Shifts**: Investors prioritize efficiency and proven use cases over raw scale.
- **Market Volatility**: Defensible, value-driven projects become paramount.

*Your roadmap needs to reflect this.*


## Operational Resilience — *noun.*

The capacity of an AI system or organization to maintain core functions and adapt effectively amidst significant external disruptions, from supply shocks to market shifts.

> Diversifying cloud providers and sourcing chips from multiple regions.


## Who Needs To Operationalize This Now

- **Founders** — Extend runway. Focus on core value. Validate product before aggressive scaling.
- **Dev Teams** — Optimize existing models. Prioritize efficiency gains over raw compute growth.
- **Operators** — Audit hardware dependencies. Explore multi-region deployments. Secure alternative vendors.


## 02. Your Next Steps By Friday

Audit your current hardware supply chain. Identify single points of failure. Start a conversation with your finance team about extending runway and securing capital in a tighter market. This is about *shipping*, not just speculating.
