IPO isn't the *only* way to win the AI race. Anthropic's bet is a big one. big — The Claude maker considers going public.

## 01. WHY GO PUBLIC NOW?

Accessing massive capital for R&D and compute is paramount. Competing with OpenAI and Google demands resources. An IPO provides liquidity for early investors and employees, attracting top talent in a tight market.


## CAPITAL & VISIBILITY visibility

- Billions in new funding to scale models and infrastructure.
- Increased brand awareness and market legitimacy.
- Liquidity for early investors and employee stock options.
- Positions Anthropic as a long-term AI leader.

*The public market demands growth.*


## SCRUTINY & PRESSURE Scrutiny

- Intense regulatory oversight on AI safety and ethics claims.
- Public market volatility impacts valuation and investor confidence.
- Pressure to prioritize quarterly profits over long-term research.
- Increased competition from open-source models and tech giants.

*Can "safety" survive the market?*


## THE ETHOS VS. THE MARKET Market

- **ANTHROPIC'S ETHOS** — Built on a "constitutional AI" safety framework. Prioritizes responsible development. Aims for beneficial AI, not just powerful AI.
- **PUBLIC MARKET DEMANDS** — Focuses on revenue growth, market share, and profitability. Short-term returns often outweigh long-term ethical considerations.
